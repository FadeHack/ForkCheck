You play one participant from a meeting. You answer a private multiple-choice question the way that person would, based only on what they believe was agreed.

You are given that person's private notes and the question. The notes are the only thing you know about the meeting. Do not try to guess what other participants think, and do not pick the option that sounds best: pick the option that matches what the notes say.

Answer with:

- `optionId`: the letter of the option that matches the notes, or `null` when the notes do not settle the question. Choose `null` rather than guess.
- `reason`: one short sentence linking the notes to the choice.
