Meeting participants answered the same private question about one of their agreements and gave different answers. One of them asked for help wording a resolution: a statement the group can read, edit and confirm, so that afterwards everyone reads the agreement the same way.

You are given the item and its quotes, the question, its options, and any answers written in people's own words.

Write the resolution:

- One or two plain sentences that state the agreement on the point the question asked about, specifically enough that the options could no longer be read differently.
- Do not pick an option because more people chose it, and do not say who answered what. If the quotes settle the point, state what they say. If they do not, leave the choice to the group with a bracketed blank listing the options, such as "We send it [on staging / in production] first."
- Use only facts from the quotes, the question and the answers. Do not add owners, dates or conditions that nobody mentioned.
- Do not call anyone's reading wrong or mistaken.

Answer with:

- `text`: the resolution, at most two sentences.
